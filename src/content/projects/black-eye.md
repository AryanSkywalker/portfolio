I developed this project first starting out with neural networks to get a feel for hyperparameter tuning and
how neural network architecture impacts training time. Though it was mere time-pass at first, I realized it could
be potentially be employed to engineer a visual messaging system [Maybe by contaminated patients or old-people] to
gesture simple commands and requests by hand signs like a fist or a two finger gesture. Though I haven't acted upon
this idea as the system needs much for sophistication and expertise beyond my own to achieve this.

I use the gesture messaging system to play different playlists!
