This was a post hello world project modification I made so as to enhance my skills and just pass time. Training on the MNIST
dataset was quite easy however due to the challenge of overfitting the problem got compounded to data augmentation to increase
the variety by over 5 folds. After long hours of training the model was able to recognize handwritten digits and operators.
Then it was simple a matter of image processing to extract the region of interest, recognize the expression and do arithmetic.

Maybe this project can be expanded to perform algebraic computation... [Just A Thought]
