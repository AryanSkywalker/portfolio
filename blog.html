A Peculiar Trait

Ursus Arctos, commonly known as the brown bear, belongs to the family Ursidae. The brown bear lives primarily in the forests of Eurasia and North America. It is among the largest land carnivores found on Earth, rivalled only by its close cousin, the polar bear.

All members of the Ursidae family are ferocious beings, except the cutie Panda. Also interesting to note is that they have a relatively large brain, and many show cognitive abilities like tool use that are akin to humans. Though they are solitary animals, many do gather near riverbanks where there is a dearth of salmon. In all, it is an exotic family with many many qualities that just can't all be put here. However, I digress.

There is one thing that they do that has caught my mind. What is important to keep in mind when I tell you this is that bears have hyperactive senses to keep out of danger. Their noses can smell blood from miles away. Their roar, described by observers as ‘thunderous’ can be heard within a kilometer. When confronted there, fake charges are enough to scare away other predators. Their hibernation isn’t as depicted in popular media. Infact, they can wake from deep sleep within seconds when in danger. Thus, it is safe to say that bears are vigilant, dominating hypercarnivores of their domain. But what I find interesting is this,

It is rare, but sometimes, in the wild, perhaps when they’re certain that no danger is nearby, bears can be found sitting. Yes, sitting -— not to chew food, or scratch their butt or even sleep. Just sitting. Sitting near the riverbank, a cliff and even with other bears. And for a long time ecologists and bear ‘experts’ haven’t been able to place why they do this. We can discuss this at length, it is quite fun to speculate and come up with our own reasons. Maybe they do it to cool off, or to catch prey off guard? What’s also funny is that a human doing something similar would tick any sane person’s mind off. Is he depressed? Did his wife leave him? A bad day at work perhaps. We can ramble on, but most will agree this is peculiar. A peculiar trait.

I stand to say that most of these so-called experts are idiots. Because I feel that you can attribute this trait to a very simple reason. Bears do this because they’re taking a break. They can be found sitting just like that because for some moments they too like to zoom out and admire nature. We know they’re large brains permit them elevated cognitive thinking. And admiration isn’t a complex emotion to emulate either. What I find so confusing about this is that almost everyone finds this strange. Well, I find this opinion strange! What is so strange and alien about sitting down, anywhere it may be. Infact, I find it endearing that even a hypercarnivore, with 50 reasons to stay alert and vigilant, does make out time to sit… and admire the wonderful view around them. I find it beautiful even… that bears do that.
